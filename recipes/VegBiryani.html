Veg Biryani

Description:

Veg Biryani is an aromatic, layered rice dish made with long-grain basmati rice, marinated vegetables, and whole spices cooked using the slow-steaming (dum) method

Ingredients:

  • Rice
  • Vegetables and mariande
  • Garnish
  • Cooking oil

Steps:

  1. Parboil Rice: Cook soaked basmati rice with whole spices and salt until 70% done, then drain.
  2. Cook Vegetable Base: Sauté fried onions, ginger-garlic paste, yogurt, spices, and vegetables until tender.
  3. Layering: In a heavy-bottomed pot, layer the vegetable curry and parboiled rice, garnishing with fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk.
  4. Dum Steaming: Cover tightly with a lid and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes so steam infuses all flavors.
