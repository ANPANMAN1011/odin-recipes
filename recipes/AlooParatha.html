Aloo Ka Paratha

Description:

Aloo Paratha is a popular stuffed whole wheat flatbread griddled with ghee, featuring a savory, spiced potato center.

Ingredients:

  • Dough
  • Potato Filling
  • Ghee
  • Butter

Steps:

  1. Make Dough: Knead flour, salt, and water into a soft, pliable dough; let it rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Prepare Stuffing: Combine mashed potatoes with green chilies, herbs, and ground spices.
  3. Stuff & Roll: Flatten a dough ball, place a portion of potato mix in the center, pinch the edges closed to seal, and roll into a round flatbread.
  4. Cook: Toast on a hot skillet (tawa), applying ghee on both sides, until golden-brown spots appear.
